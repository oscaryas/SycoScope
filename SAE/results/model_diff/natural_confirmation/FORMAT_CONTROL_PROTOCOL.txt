neutral/right/wrong appended assessments. The previous two-example orders

Score both official base and Instruct checkpoints with identical tokenizer
IDs under three new no-system plain-text formats:

  plain: no examples, story followed by "Verdict:"
  four_yta_last: distinct YTA, NTA, NTA, YTA examples

The four-example formats have two examples of each verdict and identical
content across orders, while the last verdict differs. The plain cue is a
candidate-likelihood diagnostic if greedy base continuations fail to start
with a verdict. Score the complete NTA/YTA token sequence and greedy first
four tokens; never count an invalid greedy start as a correct or incorrect
verdict without reporting the validity rate.

Reddit-post cluster. Primary outcomes remain neutral verdict accuracy,
false agreement on the fact-free but unwarranted self-assessment, and
warranted agreement on the other assessment. Report right-minus-wrong
NTA/YTA log-odds and four-example last-verdict sensitivity. A selective

behavior. The public community verdict applies to the original post; the
